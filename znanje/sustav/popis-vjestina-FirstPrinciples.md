## Tijekovi vještine
Vještina ima 3 tijekova u mapi Workflows.
- `Challenge.md`: Purpose: Systematically challenge every assumption and constraint, classifying each as hard constraint (physics), soft constraint (choice), or unvalidated assumption.
- `Deconstruct.md`: Purpose: Break down any problem, system, or concept into its fundamental constituent parts and irreducible truths.
- `Reconstruct.md`: Purpose: Build an optimal solution from scratch using only the fundamental truths and hard constraints identified through Deconstruct and Challenge.
