# Vještina Development

Vještina Development: Development standards for PAI/REGOČ agents — stack, methodology, and testing discipline.
Koristi se kad: writing or reviewing code, choosing tools, implementing features, or testing. Engineer and Architect agents load this as required knowledge.
Status u katalogu alata je kanonski, vlasnik stribor.
Dodijeljena je agentima: Stribor, Kosjenka i Jelena.
Poziva se alatom Skill (`Development`) ili čitanjem `~/.claude/skills/Development/SKILL.md`.
Vještina nema vlastitih tijekova (mapa Workflows ne postoji ili je prazna).
