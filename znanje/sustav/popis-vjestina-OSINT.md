# Vještina OSINT

Vještina OSINT: Open source intelligence gathering.
Koristi se kad: OSINT, due diligence, background check, research person, company intel, investigate.
Status u katalogu alata je kanonski, vlasnik malik.
Dodijeljena je agentima: Malik i Manda.
Poziva se alatom Skill (`OSINT`) ili čitanjem `~/.claude/skills/OSINT/SKILL.md`.

## Tijekovi vještine
Vještina ima 4 tijekova u mapi Workflows.
- `CompanyDueDiligence.md`: Purpose: Comprehensive 5-phase investment vetting combining domain-first OSINT, technical reconnaissance, multi-source research, and investment risk assessment.
- `CompanyLookup.md`: Purpose: Comprehensive business intelligence gathering for authorized research, due diligence, or security assessments.
- `EntityLookup.md`: Purpose: Technical intelligence gathering on domains, IPs, infrastructure, and threat entities.
- `PeopleLookup.md`: Purpose: Ethical open-source intelligence gathering on individuals for authorized professional contexts.
